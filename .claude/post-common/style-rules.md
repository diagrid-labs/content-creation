# Style Rules

These rules apply to every post regardless of content type or document type.

## Writing style

1. Use second-person perspective.
2. Do not use em dashes.
3. Do not use bold text formatting inside list items.
4. Do not use emojis inside list items.
5. Do not use these words: journey, dive, delve into, jump into, pivotal, underscore, harness, realm, illuminate, master.

## Title rules

1. Do not combine two sentences with a colon.
   - Bad: "Conduct Your Microservices: A Symphony of Durable Workflows"
   - Good: "Conduct Your Microservices Like a Symphony Orchestra"
2. Do not use the "From ... To ..." structure.
   - Bad: "From Microservice Chaos to Serenity with Dapr"
   - Good: "Turning Microservice Chaos into Serenity with Dapr"

## Length

The post body (intro paragraph through summary paragraph) must fall within ±15% of the target word count chosen for the post. See [post-length.md](post-length.md) for targets per document type.
