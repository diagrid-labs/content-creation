# Post Length

Target word-count ranges by document type. The measured length is the post body: from the opening intro paragraph through the closing summary paragraph. Front-matter, prompt metadata, titles, and HTML-comment guidance are excluded from the count.

| Document type | Short | Medium | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial | 800 | 1500 | 2500 |
| How-to | 500 | 900 | 1400 |
| Explanation | 700 | 1200 | 1800 |
| Reference | 500 | 1000 | 2000 |
| Product announcement | 400 | 700 | 1100 |
| Event announcement | 300 | 500 | 800 |
| Case study | 600 | 1000 | 1500 |
| Company news | 300 | 500 | 800 |
| Opinion / Thought leadership | 600 | 1000 | 1500 |

The finished post body must fall within ±15% of the chosen target.
